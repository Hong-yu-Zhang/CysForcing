Predict reactive cysteines

Give a UniProt accession or an AlphaFold2 model. CysForcing reads the sequence with ESM-2 and the structure with SaProt, and scores every cysteine.

Examples

The AlphaFold DB model of the accession is fetched by the server.

Use a full-length predicted model (AlphaFold2, as in the paper); experimental structures with missing residues are rejected.

The first 1,023 residues are scored, as in simple_inference.py.
